Engagement structure & investment

How a JK engagement works

Five phases, ninety days, one quantified sprint. Here's exactly what happens after the diagnostic — and what it costs.

The five phases

From diagnosis to delivered value

Entry diagnostic · Phases 1–2

USD 15,000 – 22,000

Fourteen days to a quantified baseline: where the money is going, sized in currency rather than adjectives. Stands alone, and is credited in full against the sprint if you go on.

Full sprint · Phases 1–5

USD 45,000 – 85,000

Ninety days from diagnosis to verified results, by fleet size and scope. Scoped precisely after the entry diagnostic — not a generic day-rate.

Fee structure

50 / 25 / 25

50% on contract signature, 25% on board presentation (end of Phase 4), 25% on the Day-90 results report. The entry diagnostic is 50 / 50 on signature and report.

Engagement guarantee

3× Year-1 ROI

If a JK engagement does not identify a minimum 3× return on investment in Year 1, we extend sprint support at no additional charge. Applies to carriers with 3+ aircraft operating scheduled or contract services upon completion of Phase 2 data verification.
