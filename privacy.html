1. Who we are (data controller)

This site is operated by JK & Associates ("JK", "we", "us", "our"), an aviation management consultancy based in Nairobi, Kenya. For the purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019, the "Act"), JK is the data controller for the limited personal data described in this notice.

  • Registered entity: JK & Associates — incorporation in progress; registration number to follow. Until incorporation completes, JK & Associates is the practice name under which this site is operated, and the obligations in this notice are accepted under that name. This entry will carry the registered name and company number once the certificate issues.
  • Office address: 2nd Avenue, Cargo Terminal, JKIA, Nairobi, Kenya
  • Email: info@aviationhubkenya.org
  • Contact for data matters: info@aviationhubkenya.org. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer (section 24 of the Act makes the appointment optional for a practice of this kind); data-protection queries are handled by JK directly at that address.
  • ODPC registration: JK is not currently registered as a data controller with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. Under regulation 13 of the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021, registration is mandatory for a controller with an annual turnover above KES 5 million or more than ten employees, or one processing data for a purpose listed in the Third Schedule to those Regulations. Advisory work of the kind described here is not a listed purpose. We will register when the requirement applies to us. Being unregistered does not narrow your rights under the Act or our duties under it: both apply in full.

2. How the tools handle your data

Every tool on this site is a client-side tool. Each one runs as code in your browser. Any values you enter are processed on your device and, where you would otherwise lose them on refresh, are saved to your browser's local storage (see section 5). This applies without exception to every tool on the tools page, to the Airline Health Scorecard, and to the Kenya Aviation Regulatory Index.

This matters most where you type things about real people and real money: the names of proposed shareholders in the Corporate Structure Designer, the names of proposed postholders in the Organogram Planner, and your capital, revenue and funding assumptions in the venture and returns tools. Those stay on your device.

  • Running a tool transmits nothing. There is no account, no upload, and no background submission. You can complete every diagnostic on this site without sending us anything.
  • We use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking technologies, and no social-media pixels.
  • We cannot access, view, link or sell anything you have not sent us. Until you press one of the send buttons described immediately below, you have not sent us anything.

The exceptions, in full

Some optional forms on this site do send data to us. We set them out exactly here, so that the paragraph above is not read as more absolute than it is. Each one requires you to fill it in and press a button; none fires on its own, and nothing is queued or sent later.

  • “Request the 90-Day Roadmap Briefing” on the scorecard results page sends your name, email address, airline, fleet size, your health index, your three weakest domains with their scores, and a link to your report.
  • “Request a debrief” on the results page sends your name, email address, airline, role, preferred week, and the same health index, weakest domains and report link.
  • Tool enquiry forms at the end of each tool send which tool you were using, plus your name, email address, organisation and the primary concern you select. They do not send the figures, names or answers you entered in the tool itself.

One point deserves emphasis, because it is easy to miss. The report link in the first two forms carries your scorecard answers encoded in the address itself. Sending it therefore sends us your answers, not merely your score. That is what makes a useful debrief possible, since a consultant who can see which options you selected can prepare properly. It is still your decision, and you should make it knowing that is what the link contains. If you would rather we saw only the headline figure, email us instead and paste in what you choose.

These submissions are received through Netlify Forms and stored in our Netlify account (see section 6). They are not fed into any analytics, advertising or profiling system, they are not used for automated decisions, and they are not shared with anyone outside JK.

3. Personal data we process

Given the design above, the only personal data involved is:

(a) Information you send us

If you use a "contact" or email link, your own email application opens a draft addressed to us. If you send that email, we receive your email address, your name (if you include it) and whatever you choose to write, which may include information about your airline. You decide what to share.

If instead you submit one of the forms listed in section 2, we receive the fields named there. For the two results-page forms, that includes your health index, your weakest domains and a report link that carries your scorecard answers. Those forms are the only route by which anything you typed into a diagnostic can reach us, and each one requires an explicit submission.

Please send us only what you are entitled to share. If you name colleagues, shareholders or candidates in an email, make sure you may pass their details to an adviser.

(b) Technical data handled by our service providers

As with any website, when your browser loads our pages our hosting and content-delivery provider automatically processes limited technical data (such as your IP address, the page requested, browser type and timestamp) to deliver the page and keep it secure. We do not use this data to identify you and we do not build profiles from it. See sections 6 and 7.

Our typefaces are served from this same domain, so loading a page discloses your IP address only to our hosting provider and to no one else.

We do not ask for, and the tools are not designed to hold, sensitive personal data as defined in the Act (such as health, biometric or family details). Please do not put it in a form or an email.

4. Purposes and lawful bases

Under section 30 of the Act we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis:

  • Responding to your enquiry, preparing a briefing or debrief you asked for, and taking steps toward a possible engagement: your consent in choosing to send it to us, and the necessity of taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Delivering and securing the website (technical data handled by providers): our legitimate interest in operating a safe, functioning website, balanced against your rights.
  • Keeping records we are required to keep once an engagement begins (for example, accounting and tax records): compliance with a legal obligation.

We do not use what you send us for direct marketing or add you to a mailing list. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us; this does not affect processing already carried out.

5. Local storage on your device

To make the tools usable, your browser stores your entries under keys beginning jk_ or dn_, for example jk_airline_scorecard_v3, jk_structure_v3, jk_organogram_v3, jk_venture_file_v3 and dn_operating_model_canvas_v1. This is local storage, not a cookie: it stays in your browser, is not sent in network requests, and is not visible to us. You remain in control:

  • Use the "Reset" / "Clear" buttons in the tools, or
  • Clear your browser's site data for this domain, or
  • Use a private/incognito window, which discards it when you close the window.

Exporting your workspace

The Venture Control Room can export everything the tools have saved (the whole workspace, including any names and financial assumptions you entered) into a single .json file that your browser downloads to your device. We do not receive that file and it is not uploaded anywhere. Two things follow, and you should weigh both:

  • The file is a plain, readable copy of your inputs. Treat it with the same care as any other confidential document; it is only as protected as wherever you save it.
  • Once you send it to someone (a colleague, a lender, an adviser), it is outside our design and outside our control. Nothing in this notice can protect data you have chosen to forward.

Importing a workspace file replaces what the tools currently hold on that device. The import runs entirely in your browser and reads only keys belonging to this site.

6. Who else is involved (processors & third parties)

We keep third parties to the minimum needed to serve a static website:

  • Hosting and content delivery: Netlify hosts the static files and serves them over HTTPS.
  • Form submissions: the optional forms described in section 2 are received and stored by Netlify Forms, part of the same hosting provider. This is the only place data you send through this site is held. Nothing reaches it unless you press one of those send buttons.
  • Web fonts: none. Our typefaces are self-hosted on this domain, and no third-party font, script, analytics or advertising service is loaded anywhere on this site.
  • Email: if you email us, or we reply to a form, our email provider processes that message so we can read and answer it.

These providers act as our processors, or as independent controllers of the technical data described above. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share your tool inputs with anyone because we never receive them. If a regulator or court lawfully requires us to disclose information, we will disclose only what is required.

7. International data transfers

Our hosting and content-delivery provider operates infrastructure outside Kenya, including in the United States and other countries, and form submissions are stored on that infrastructure. Loading the site, submitting a form and emailing us may therefore involve transferring the limited data described above outside Kenya. Under sections 48 and 49 of the Act, such transfers are made in reliance on the provider's own data-protection commitments and contractual safeguards. If you would like more detail on a specific provider's safeguards, contact us.

8. Retention

Section 39 of the Act requires us to keep personal data no longer than is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for. In practice:

  • Your tool inputs: retained only on your device, for as long as you keep them, unless you submitted one of the forms in section 2. In that case the fields that form sends, including the report link carrying your answers, are held with the enquiry data below.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: kept while the conversation is live and for a reasonable period afterwards, in case you return to it, then deleted or anonymised. You can ask us to delete yours at any time (section 9).
  • Engagement records: kept for the life of the engagement and then for as long as Kenyan accounting, tax and limitation rules require.
  • Provider technical logs: retained by those providers under their own retention schedules.

9. Your rights, and how quickly we answer

Section 26 of the Act gives you the right to:

  • be informed of the use to which your personal data is to be put;
  • access your personal data in our custody;
  • object to the processing of all or part of your personal data;
  • correction of false or misleading data; and
  • deletion of false or misleading data about you.

The Act also gives you the rights to restrict processing (section 34), to data portability (section 38), to rectification and erasure (section 40), and not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (section 35). Our tools score only the figures you enter, on your device, and produce indicative guidance; they make no decision about you.

10. If something goes wrong

If personal data we hold is accessed or acquired by someone not authorised to have it, and there is a real risk of harm to you, section 43 of the Act requires us to notify the Data Commissioner without delay and within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of it, and to tell you in writing within a reasonably practicable time, with enough information for you to protect yourself. We will meet that duty. Because the tools hold your inputs only on your device, the data that could be affected is limited to what you have sent us.

11. Complaints to the ODPC

If you believe we have not handled your personal data lawfully, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right, under section 56 of the Act, to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya: website www.odpc.go.ke, email info@odpc.go.ke.

12. Children

This site is a professional tool intended for airline executives, investors and their advisers, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of anyone under 18.

13. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with security headers, including a content-security policy that restricts which scripts may run and which sites may embed our pages. Because your tool inputs never leave your device and we operate no central store of them, the most sensitive data simply never reaches us. What you do send is held in our Netlify and email accounts, which are protected by our providers' security measures and limited to JK.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows the current version. If a change affects how we use data you have already sent us, we will tell you before it takes effect.

15. How to contact us

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact info@aviationhubkenya.org, or write to us at the office address in section 1. Both reach the same people; email is faster.
