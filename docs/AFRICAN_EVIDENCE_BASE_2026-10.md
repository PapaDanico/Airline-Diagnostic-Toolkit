Every finding is dated, sourced and graded by source quality: **A** official or primary; **B** credible trade or institutional reporting; **C** indicative, single-source, or from another market.

**Status: leads, not certificates.** Sources were reached through search results and not read in their primary text. Nothing here has been added to `assets/js/data.js` benchmarks: `scripts/check-data.mjs` requires a source and date on every benchmark, and a finding enters that file only after it is confirmed from its primary document.
