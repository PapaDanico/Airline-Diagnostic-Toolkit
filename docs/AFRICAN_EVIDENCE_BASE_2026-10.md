# African business-aviation evidence base — October 2026

Public evidence gathered by web search on 23 October 2026 to ground the platform's planning defaults and the advisory work built on it. Every finding is dated, sourced and graded by source quality: **A** official or primary; **B** credible trade or institutional reporting; **C** indicative, single-source, or from another market.

**Status: leads, not certificates.** Sources were reached through search results and not read in their primary text. Nothing here has been added to `assets/js/data.js` benchmarks: `scripts/check-data.mjs` requires a source and date on every benchmark, and a finding enters that file only after it is confirmed from its primary document.
