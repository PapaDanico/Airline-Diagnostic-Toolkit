Kenya Aviation Regulatory Index

Every requirement in the JK toolkit carries a citation. This is the index behind them: the instrument, its Legal Notice number, where to read it, which sectors it binds — and, for each one, whether we could confirm it against the public gazette record or not.

— unconfirmed, and marked as such
A regulation titled "…Regulations, 2025" carries a 2026 Legal Notice number Kenya's revised civil aviation regulations were made in 2025 and gazetted through 2026. The instrument keeps its 2025 title; the Legal Notice takes the year it was published. Citing one without the other is how applications end up quoting a regulation that does not exist. Cite both, every time.
