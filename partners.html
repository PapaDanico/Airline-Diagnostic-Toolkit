Qualified conversations

The gap report prescribes next steps that map to your product category as well as JK's engagement phases — so the client arrives at your sales conversation already knowing why they need it.

Who it fits. Analytics and revenue-management vendors, MRO software providers, lessors doing portfolio health checks, regional airline associations, and consultancies that want a neutral diagnostic front-end. The scorecard is deliberately tool-agnostic, and the boundary is a real one rather than a courtesy: JK licenses no fleet, schedule or demand data. Every figure in these tools is either supplied by the operator or a published reference we cite by name. Platforms like Cirium, AviAnalysis and Maureva sell the data; this asks the operator the question.
