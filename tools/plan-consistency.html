Plan Consistency Check

A plan that contradicts itself cannot be approved, and a Board will find the contradiction in the meeting rather than before it. Enter what the plan says — the launch date on one page, the launch date on another, the fleet count, the headcount chart and its headline — and this finds the same fact stated two ways and the totals that do not add up. In a recent 37-page board deck it found thirteen in an afternoon.

Step 1

What does the plan say?

One row per statement. Give each fact the same topic wherever it appears — "Launch date", "Fleet size", "FY2027 headcount" — with the value as written and where it is stated. Numbers, dates and words are all compared; two rows on one topic that disagree are a finding.

Step 2

Do the totals add up?

List the parts of a total, separated by commas, and the total the plan states. Revenue lines against the revenue headline; a headcount chart against its ceiling; phased spend against the budget.

A total within this share of its parts counts as adding up.
