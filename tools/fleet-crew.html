Fleet & Crew Planner

A fleet plan usually stops at the aircraft list, and the headcount that follows it is a ceiling nobody derived. Crew do not scale with tails: a pilot is rated on one type, so each type is its own pool and rounds up on its own, and a single-tail type has no one to cover it. Enter the entry-into-service list and the operating concept; this works out aircraft-years, the pilots each type pool needs, and what a round-the-clock control room costs in people.
