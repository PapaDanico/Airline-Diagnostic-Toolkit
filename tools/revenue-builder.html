Assumptions and limits

Rate bands are JK & Associates planning ranges, informed by public broker, press and operator guidance on charter pricing, management fees, jet cards, MRO labour rates, FBO handling and aerial-survey rates. They are unverified planning estimates, not citations, and none is a quotation. Flight streams earn rate × hours; non-flight streams earn price × volume, or a percentage of a base you enter, and carry their own direct cost. Seasonality is applied to Year 1 only using monthly index factors; Years 2–10 use a flat annual average. No discounting — all figures are nominal. Break-even hours are computed as annual capital charge ÷ (rate − DOC); for leased aircraft the annual lease rental replaces the capital charge.

This tool is a planning aid, not investment advice, a valuation, or a substitute for a bankable financial model. Obtain independent financial and legal advice before committing capital.

A revenue model that survives diligence looks different from this.

This tests whether the fleet can service its capital at indicative rates. A defensible revenue case needs a real demand study, contracted anchor clients and a yield model that holds under utilisation stress.
