Assumptions and limits

Postholder sets follow the posts an authority ordinarily requires for each organisation type under ICAO-derived frameworks; deputy and key-manager-absence expectations follow established European and ICAO guidance widely applied across African authorities. Names entered here stay in this browser and are never transmitted.

A planning aid, not regulatory advice. The definitive list of required posts, their acceptance criteria and any deputisation requirement is set by the applicable regulations and the Authority's advisory circulars for your specific application. Confirm before you nominate.
